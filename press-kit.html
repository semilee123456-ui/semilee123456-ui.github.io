How much of a US lottery jackpot do winners actually keep?

ChamTax (참택스) is a free calculator that shows the after-tax take-home amount of a US Powerball or Mega Millions jackpot, for 21 different countries of residence, in 26 languages. This page is a quick reference for journalists and bloggers — feel free to use the table below in your coverage.

Reference scenario: a large lump-sum jackpot (~$100M USD). US federal withholding (30%, or graduated up to 37% for US residents) is applied first, then each country's own tax treatment of foreign lottery income — after any Foreign Tax Credit the country's law/treaty allows. Numbers come directly from ChamTax's live calculation engine, not manual estimates re-typed by hand.
